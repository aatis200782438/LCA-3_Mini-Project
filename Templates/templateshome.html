Where this data comes from

  • Affidavits (assets, cases, education, age): self-sworn Form 26 filings compiled by ADR / MyNeta Maharashtra 2024.
  • Results (votes, margins, runner-ups): Election Commission of India counts, 2024 general election (20 Nov 2024).
  • Cross-check: ADR winners' analysis (Nov 2024) — 277 of 286 processed winners crorepatis, 188 with declared cases, 116 with serious cases.
  • Two partial records: Awhad (Mumbra-Kalwa) and Jankar (Malshiras) — their affidavit summaries are missing from ADR/MyNeta's processed lists; assets shown from news reports of their filings, with last-known 2019 declarations noted on their profiles.
  • Bye-elections: Baramati & Rahuri were re-contested in 2026 — both current MLAs and both results are shown.
