How we work

Every number on this portal is traceable to a public record. This page explains what we show, where each part comes from, and the rules we hold ourselves to.

1 · Candidate profiles come from sworn affidavits

Assets, income, criminal cases, education and age are self-declared in Form 26 filed with nomination papers. We do not retype them — we link each MLA to their affidavit record on MyNeta (ADR) and the ECI Affidavit Portal, which host the scanned originals. A declared case is not a conviction.

2 · Funds are our core work — allocated vs actually spent

How "actually spent" is counted:

Money is counted as actually spent only when the work it funded is completed or near completion. Sanctioned, tendered or under-construction amounts are shown as Ambiguous, never as spent.

Sources: state government resolutions (GRs) for the MLA Local Area Development Fund ceilings, utilisation certificates, District Planning Committee records and RTI replies. Since September 2025 the state's e-Samarth platform (modelled on the Centre's e-Sakshi for MPs) digitises MLA-fund proposals and payments — Pune is the pilot district.

3 · Works are tracked to completion

Works come from municipal and District Planning Committee records and completion certificates. News reports are used as leads to verify, never as proof of completion.

4 · Promises are matched to delivery

Each election manifesto ("Sankalpanama") is itemised and every item tracked: fulfilled, in progress, carried forward, or no public update found. "No public update found" means exactly that — not a judgement.

Our rules

1
No source, no numberAnything we cannot trace to a public record is marked as not traced rather than estimated.
2
Declared ≠ convictedCriminal-case counts are self-declared affidavit data, shown as filed, with status where public.
3
No ratings, no ranking, no endorsementWe present verified facts side by side; the voter concludes.
4
Corrections welcomeIf a figure or status is wrong, we fix it and note the correction.
